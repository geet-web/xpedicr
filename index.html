About Xpedicr

A Scalable Ecosystem for a Healthier India

Xpedicr Industry Pvt. Ltd. is a premier HealthTech parent company dedicated to creating scalable, end-to-end healthcare solutions across India.

We are integrating fragmented medical services into a unified, technology-driven platform that ensures accessibility, affordability, and rapid response for every citizen.

Our Vision

To make healthcare faster, smarter, and accessible to every Indian, creating a future where geography and infrastructure never stand in the way of saving lives.

Our Mission

To integrate technology, healthcare providers, emergency support, and wellness services into one seamless, trustworthy ecosystem that empowers patients and providers alike.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Xpedicr Industry Pvt. Ltd., we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our healthcare services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal identification information, including but not limited to your name, email address, phone number, and medical history when you register for an account, book a service, or consult with our partners.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is primarily used to provide you with rapid emergency responses, accurate AI-assisted diagnostics via Nava.ai, and seamless telemedicine consultations. We also use aggregated data to improve our service logistics.

3. Data Security

We implement state-of-the-art encryption and robust secure server architecture to protect your personal health information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We only share necessary information with verified partner clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies directly involved in your care. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.
