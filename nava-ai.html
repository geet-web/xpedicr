What is Nava.ai

The Brain Behind Every Healthcare Decision

Nava.ai is Xpedicr's proprietary AI intelligence platform — a real-time decision engine trained on India's unique healthcare demand patterns. It sits at the core of every service in the Xpedicr ecosystem, from pharmacy fulfilment to emergency ambulance dispatch.

Unlike generic AI tools, Nava.ai is purpose-built for the speed, complexity, and diversity of Indian healthcare — handling multi-language inputs, regional hospital capacity data, and live traffic routing simultaneously.
